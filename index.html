Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the CMS Student application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. CMS Student is part of a college/university Campus Management System (CMS) that lets enrolled students view their academic information after their account has been linked to their official student record by their institution's administration.

Information We Collect

To provide the App's features, we collect and process the following types of information:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Account & identity information Name, email address, CNIC/registration number, date of birth, roll number, department and session To verify your identity and link your account to your official student record
Academic information Class timetable, attendance records, exam results, grades/GPA, submitted exam papers To display your personal academic dashboard, schedule, and results
Usage & device information App version, device type, crash/diagnostic logs To maintain app stability and fix bugs
Authentication data Login credentials (handled by our authentication provider), session tokens To securely sign you in and keep your session active

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and verify your student account and link it to the correct academic record
  • To display your class schedule, calendar, attendance, and exam/results information
  • To allow your institution's teachers and administrators to manage academic records relevant to you
  • To send you academic notifications and calendar events relevant to your department, session, and shift
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the App

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your academic and identity information is visible only to:

  • You, the student the record belongs to
  • Authorized teachers and administrative staff of your institution who require it to perform their academic duties
  • Our backend infrastructure provider (Supabase), which stores data securely on our behalf and does not use it for any purpose other than providing the service to us

We do not share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure, access-controlled servers. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures, including authentication and role-based access control, to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Retention

We retain your academic and account information for as long as your account is active or as required to fulfil academic record-keeping obligations of your institution. You may request that your account be delinked from the App at any time by contacting your institution's administration.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can access your own academic information at any time within the App
  • You can request correction of inaccurate personal information through your institution's administration
  • You can request that your account be unlinked or deleted by contacting us using the details below

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by enrolled college/university students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
